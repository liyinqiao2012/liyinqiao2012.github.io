---
title: "Elected as a chair of WiNLP (ACL-affiliated affinity group) 🎒"
date: 2025-05-31
---
I am elected to be a chair of WiNLP (ACL-affiliated affinity group) which aims to help promote and support ideas and voices of underrepresented groups in the NLP community. I will support and organize the WiNLP 2025 workshop in conjunction with EMNLP 2025 in Suzhou, China.
