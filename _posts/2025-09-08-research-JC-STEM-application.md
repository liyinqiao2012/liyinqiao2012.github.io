Submitted a research project application to Jockey Club STEM Early Career Research Fellowship for Translation and Application. This is the first project application I have led, including both the initial idea and the drafting of the content. Although only 10 projects will be funded in Hong Kong and the competition is extremely intense, I am proud of the proposal I developed and will continue pursuing research along these lines. The project focuses on the low-resource task adaptation for LLMs, including data augmentation, multi-task learning, knowledge verification, etc. More details will be available after acceptance. Best wishes to my application!
